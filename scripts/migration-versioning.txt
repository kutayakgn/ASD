Migration gelistirme girisi
==========================
Tek guncel rehber ve zorunlu sozlesme: docs/ef-core-migrations-contract.md

1. Entity ve Data/Configurations/EntityConfigurations.cs icindeki ilgili sinifi duzenle.
2. SQL is kurali varsa Migrations/DatabaseObjects altindaki dosyayi duzenle.
3. powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File scripts/migration.ps1 add ChangeName
4. powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File scripts/test-migration-deployment.ps1
5. Normal deploy. Uygulama bekleyen EF migration'larini uygular.

EF: standart .cs/.Designer.cs/snapshot. SQL: surum basina tek .sql paketi.
Gecmis paketler ve Legacy adaptorleri normal gelistirme yeri degildir.
Uygulanmis migration kaynak koddan once silinmez. Rehberdeki rollback sirasini kullan.
